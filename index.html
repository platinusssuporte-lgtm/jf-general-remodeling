Who we are

A remodeling crew that treats your home like a finished piece.

JF General Remodeling is a general contractor working across Philadelphia and the surrounding counties. We take on residential and commercial projects — a single room, a full floor, or an entire property — and we handle the work from demolition through final finish.

The difference shows up where most people stop looking: the line where the baseboard meets the floor, the seam that disappears into the drywall, the tile that lines up across the whole room. That is the part we care about most.
