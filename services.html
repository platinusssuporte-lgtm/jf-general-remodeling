Services

Six specialties, one standard of finish.

Carpentry, drywall, paint, floors and tile handled by one crew — so nobody is waiting on anybody else, and nobody is blaming the last trade for the seam that shows.

01 · Finish Carpentry and Molding

Finish Carpentry and Molding

Crown molding, door and window casing, chair rail and custom trim. We cut to the room as it actually is — walls are never as square as the plan says — so the joints stay closed after the house settles.

  • Crown molding and cove
  • Door and window casing
  • Chair rail and wainscoting
  • Custom trim and built-ins
02 · Baseboard Installation

Baseboard Installation

The line where the wall meets the floor is the one people notice without knowing why. We scribe the baseboard to the floor, miter the corners tight and caulk the top edge so nothing telegraphs through the paint.

  • MDF, pine and hardwood profiles
  • Scribed to uneven floors
  • Tight mitered and coped corners
  • Caulked and paint-ready
03 · Drywall Installation and Repair

Drywall Installation and Repair

New walls, ceilings, partitions and patches. Framing squared, board hung, seams taped and sanded through the coats it takes — not the coats that are quick — so the finished wall reads as one flat surface under raking light.

  • New walls, ceilings and partitions
  • Water damage and hole repair
  • Taping and Level 4 / 5 finish
  • Moisture-resistant board in wet areas
04 · Interior and Exterior Painting

Interior and Exterior Painting

Almost every paint complaint starts before the paint does. We patch, sand, mask and prime first, then apply quality product in even coats — and we protect your floors and furniture the whole way through.

  • Full surface prep and priming
  • Interior walls, ceilings and trim
  • Exterior siding and trim
  • Clean masking, clean cleanup
05 · Flooring Installation

Flooring Installation

We check and level the subfloor before anything goes down, because a beautiful floor over a bad subfloor is a callback waiting to happen. Then hardwood, engineered, laminate or luxury vinyl, laid so the runs line up room to room.

  • Subfloor prep and leveling
  • Hardwood and engineered wood
  • Laminate and luxury vinyl plank
  • Transitions, thresholds and trim
06 · Tile and Stone Installation

Tile and Stone Installation

Layout gets planned before the first tile is set, so the cuts land in the corners instead of at eye level. Waterproofing in showers and wet areas is done properly, because that is the part you can never fix later.

  • Bathroom and shower tile
  • Kitchen backsplash
  • Marble and natural stone
  • Waterproofing and membranes
